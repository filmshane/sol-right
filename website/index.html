How solar lowers your electric bill

Panels make power your home uses first. Every kWh you generate is a kWh you don’t buy from the utility — so the energy portion of your bill drops. Dave turns your address + bill into a plain-English savings snapshot.

1. Produce power at home

Your system generates daytime electricity matched to your usage goals (often ~80–100% offset).

2. Use solar first

Appliances and HVAC pull from the panels when the sun is up — less meter spin from the grid.

3. Buy fewer kWh

Lower kWh from the utility means lower energy charges. Fixed fees may still appear on the bill.

4. See the $ ballpark

Dave estimates monthly and multi-year savings from your bill and production model — then offers a specialist call.
